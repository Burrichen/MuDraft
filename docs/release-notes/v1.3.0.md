MuDraft 1.3.0: edit album genres, track your progress through Listen List albums, and clearer Stats.

- **Album genres.** Edit an album's genres from its page (up to 3). Your choice is kept when the album is refreshed from MusicBrainz. **Reset to default** brings back MusicBrainz's genres, even offline.
- **Listen List progress.** Albums on your Listen List have an eye toggle next to each track's heart. Use it to mark tracks you've already heard, which then turn grey. The marks are cleared when you log the album or remove it from the List, and come back if you undo.
- **Whole albums only.** A listen is now always logged for the whole album, and "Log tracks…" is gone. Partial listens you logged before stay in your history.
- **Track highlights.** Albums in your Collection show their highest and lowest rated tracks.
- **Stats.**
  - Top rated now shows the best 3 artists, genres or decades that have at least 3 rated albums.
  - Various Artists no longer counts as an artist.
  - Decade labels no longer read "1970ss".
  - The "How you listen" chart and the undated-listen notes are gone.
- **GitHub Sync.** Genre edits and Listen List marks sync between your computers. If one computer edits an album's genres and another resets them, MuDraft asks which to keep. **Update every computer to 1.3.0.** A computer still on an older version won't accept the new sync data until it's updated.
- **Upgrading** keeps your profile. On first launch, MuDraft backs up your data to the data folder's `backups`, then updates the database automatically.
- **Coming from 1.1.0?** 1.3.0 also has everything that was made for 1.2.0, which was never published: the macOS app (`docs/MACOS.md`) and optional GitHub Sync (`docs/SYNC.md`).
