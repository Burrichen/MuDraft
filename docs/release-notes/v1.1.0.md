MuDraft 1.1.0: simpler editions, Collection removal, a Liked tab, and a redesigned Stats page.

**Windows x64: unsigned personal release.** The installer has no Authenticode signature, so Windows SmartScreen or antivirus software may warn about it. Check the file against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

- **Editions, not formats.** When you add an album or another edition, each edition (Standard, Deluxe, Expanded…) appears once. The CD, vinyl, cassette, country and digital releases of the same edition are no longer listed separately. Edition names filled in automatically no longer include format or country.
- **Remove from your Collection.** Collection cards have a Remove button, select mode can remove several albums at once, and album pages have "Remove from Collection" next to "Edit details". Ratings, reviews, listens and tags are kept. Collection cards no longer show "Mark listened".
- **Liked tracks.** The heart on a track now means "Liked". A new Liked tab lists every liked track, and you can unlike tracks from there. Stats shows only a count, with a link to the tab.
- **Redesigned Stats.**
  - A headline "time listened" figure and overview tiles.
  - Charts for listening activity over the last 12 months, listens by year, a half-star rating histogram, how you listen, average rating by decade, and your highest-rated genres.
  - A decade chart and ranked genre and artist bars for your Listen List.
  - A Next Up chart showing what came of each way of picking.
  - Every chart shows values on hover and has a Table button that shows the same numbers as a table.
- Upgrading keeps your profile in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\app.mudraft.desktop`. No data migration is needed.

There is no macOS build in this release. See `docs/WINDOWS.md` for details and `docs/RELEASE_BLOCKERS.md` for known limitations.
