MuDraft 1.3.5: a simpler sidebar and cleaner Stats.

- **Liked tracks** are no longer in the sidebar. Open them from the **Liked tracks** card in Stats.
- **Stats.**
  - The first card is now **Unique albums in Collection**. Each album counts once, however many editions you have.
  - **Total listens** sits next to it and counts every listen, dated or not. It replaces the "Dated listens" card.
  - Notes about missing information are gone: undated listens, unrated albums, albums without a genre or year, and tracks of unknown length.
- **Upgrading** keeps your profile. 1.3.5 doesn't change your data, and it syncs with 1.3.0.
